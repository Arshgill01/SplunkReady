# Wave 30 - Scoring and Verdict

## Goal

Convert violations into an explainable readiness verdict.

## Scope

- Severity weights.
- Score model.
- Verdict thresholds.
- Blocker conditions.

## Files Owned

- scoring files.
- scoring tests.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Critical violations can force not-ready verdict.
- Score is reproducible from violations.
- Receipt can explain score.

## Verification

- scoring tests.

## Reviewer Checklist

- Is score defensible in 30 seconds?
- Are thresholds documented?

## Stop Conditions

- Readiness score appears arbitrary.
