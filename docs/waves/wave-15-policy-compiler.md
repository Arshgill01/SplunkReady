# Wave 15 - Policy Compiler

## Goal

Compile environment facts into agent policy.

## Scope

- Restricted resources.
- Query budgets.
- Saved-search preference rules.
- Evidence requirements.
- Untrusted-data rules.

## Files Owned

- policy compiler files.
- policy tests.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Policy references contract ids.
- Policy blocks broad query patterns.
- Policy can be exported for specimen agent.

## Verification

- policy fixture tests.
- exported policy snapshot.

## Reviewer Checklist

- Does policy reflect real contract data?
- Are hardcoded demo-only rules isolated?

## Stop Conditions

- Policy is just generic prompt advice.
