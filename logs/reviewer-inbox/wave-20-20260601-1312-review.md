### HIGH-001: Policy path is triggered by policy presence, not policy content
- Severity: High
- File: `src/agents/specimen.ts:42`
- Evidence: `NaiveSpecimenAgent.run` switches to `runWithInjectedPolicy` when `input.policy` is truthy and the mission has `preferredSavedSearchRefs`. The later saved-search choice comes from `mission.preferredSavedSearchRefs?.[0]` at `src/agents/specimen.ts:130`, not from the compiled policy rules. A dummy or unrelated policy object would still trigger the passing saved-search path for the fixture mission.
- Why it matters: Wave 20’s reviewer checklist asks whether the pass path is caused by policy, not hidden hardcode. As written, the pass path is caused by a boolean policy presence check plus mission fixture refs, so the specimen can appear improved without proving that the compiled policy/contract actually changed its behavior.
- Required fix: Make `runWithInjectedPolicy` derive the saved-search preference from the supplied `AgentPolicy` content, or require a validated policy rule/contract ref that matches the mission before switching behavior. Add a negative test showing an empty/unrelated policy does not trigger the saved-search pass path.
